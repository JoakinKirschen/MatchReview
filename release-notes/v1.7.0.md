## What's new in 1.7.0

**Scorers and assists on the lineup pictures**
- Every lineup picture in the match timeline now shows who scored (⚽) and who gave the assist (blue badge) while that lineup was on the pitch. Each goal is marked once, on the lineup that was playing when it was scored.
- Two goals or assists by one player in the same spell show a count (⚽2).
- A substitute who comes on and scores shows both the yellow "came on" highlight and the ball.
- The same markers appear in the live match timeline and in the PDF match summary.
- Screen readers read the goals and assists after each player's name.
- In French the assist badge reads P (passe décisive), in German V (Vorlage).

**Under the hood**
- Built with Kotlin 2.0; the database code is generated with KSP. Your matches, players and backups are unchanged (database version 6, backup format 2).
